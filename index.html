Chris Lee

PhD Candidate, Operations Research

Advised by Xiuli Chao & Izak Duenyas. Graduating May 2028.

Research summary

I start from operational problems such as hiring under uncertainty and demand response in energy systems that have constraints on feedback: what a decision-maker observes, when, and how much of it is shared across decisions. Solutions take the form of algorithms with regret guarantees and matching lower bounds.

I am looking for a summer 2027 internship on an applied science or research science team.
